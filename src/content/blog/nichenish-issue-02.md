## Newsletter #1 — WP More

WP More ([wpmore.net](https://wpmore.net)) crossed **1,000 Substack subscribers** with Issue 58 this week. Issue #1 said 316. The count went 316 → 385 → 453 → 518 → 541 → 663 → 734 → 780 → 878 → 979 → 1,024, one number per issue from Issue 48 to Issue 58. The biggest single jump was Issue 56, from 780 to 878.

I am keeping the email sending intentionally on Substack right now. Substack recommendation is doing great work for WP More. I am also utilising the [wpmore.net](http://wpmore.net) WordPress site; it’s constantly pulling **80-100 visitors** per issue, so there is another channel.

Since July, I started publishing two YouTube Shorts with each issue, cross-posted to Facebook, Instagram, and Threads through Meta Business Suite. I also created an All Things WordPress publication on Medium, which has 18 followers so far.

I've met the 500-subscriber goal for 2026, so I need a new target.

*How about 5,000 subscribers in the next 90 days?!*

## Newsletter #2 — Weekly NishIsHere

NishIsHere ([nishishere.com](https://nishishere.com)) Issues 103 through 108 are out. Substack is at **1,910 subscribers** as of Issue 108.

The main project has been the positioning of the intro and landing page, so "curated finds for curious minds" says something clearer to a new reader. The content itself hasn't changed.

NishNama Digest, the Bangla translation at [nishnama.com](https://nishnama.com), now also goes out as an audio and video podcast on YouTube (@NishatShahriyar) and Spotify. The email list at [nishnama.substack.com](https://nishnama.substack.com) has **11,000+ subscribers**.

HighDegree\* Marketing ([serplabseo.com/subscribe-highdegree/](https://serplabseo.com/subscribe-highdegree/)) Issues 29 through 37 are out. ## ETC #2 — What I'm doing at WPManageNinja

**Fluent Forms.** We are very close to 800K active installations, and we just released 6.2.15, which brings Apple Pay and Google Pay to Stripe payment forms, browser autofill for form fields, an Export button on the Entries page, one-click duplication of integration feeds, and an optional setting that helps AI-powered browsers understand your forms, plus key fixes and security improvements.

**FluentPlayer.** The 1.3.0 and 1.4.0 releases shipped. We are going to push the LTD this BFCM hard.

**Ninja Tables.** The main focus is the website redesign; there are lots of improvements that can be made. I believe this will push the plugin’s organic growth. On the development side, we are regularly shipping improvements.

Then I went to Nepal last week. I attended **WordCamp Kathmandu 2026** on September 18 and 19, and traveled with family. The Kathmandu WordPress community is on another level. It is one of my best WordCamp experiences, 2nd WordCamp Malaysia 2025.
