## What I watched in June - the highlights

June was heavy on YouTube. I had a lot of late-night watch sessions, a few rabbit holes I didn't see coming, and honestly, some of the best creator/business content I've come across this year. Bollywood commentary was everywhere on my feed this month — and a couple of them were genuinely worth the time. Here are the highlights from 82 June’s YouTube video list.

---

## Money, business & creators

[I documented my SaaS journey to $20K MRR](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UTjU_30SHIo) - One founder, real numbers, no fluff. He walks through exactly how he got from zero to $20K monthly recurring revenue — what worked, what didn't, and the stuff most people skip over when they talk about SaaS success.

[How to Fix Inauthentic Content Before You Start Losing Viewers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Vr1HFPVzdU) - A practical breakdown of why content starts feeling hollow over time and what actually fixes it. More useful than most "be yourself" advice you see on this topic.

[Build a Substack That Actually Makes You Money (Starting from Zero)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qiDQb4PCkCc) - Less about Substack the platform, more about the fundamentals of building a paid writing business. Good framing for anyone thinking about newsletters as a revenue stream, not just a hobby.

[How to Scale a Skincare Brand (Marketing Breakdown)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WllN2scdYCA) - A breakdown of how a DTC skincare brand grew — channels, messaging, the whole thing. The marketing principles here apply well beyond skincare; worth watching even if beauty isn't your industry.

[7 bizarrely good startups that the internet has not caught up to yet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5P6k92gr96c) - Exactly what the title says. Seven companies doing genuinely interesting things that haven't blown up yet. The kind of video you share with someone immediately after finishing it.

[$8B manager exposes the fake financial "Gurus" destroying your net worth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RP9uwr_WrQY) - An actual fund manager sitting down and going through the nonsense that financial influencers push. Blunt, specific, and refreshing.

[The only calculation Elon ran to know if SpaceX would be a hit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t3HWIey2lh8) - Short and sharp. One mental model, explained clearly. The kind of thing you think about for a while after.

[How the SpaceX IPO is creating 4000+ workers into millionaires](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Iy-O4-4w43Q) - The human side of the SpaceX IPO story — what it actually means for the people who built it. Good companion to the disaster video below.

[SpaceX Went Public — A Disaster Waiting to Happen](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FPIGu0anfAE) - A measured but sharp take on what SpaceX going public actually means — and why it might not be the win people think it is. Watched this twice.

[This guy made billions from just 3 stocks (Here's how)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6WAfCgjlcqU) - Concentration over diversification — and the rare case where it worked spectacularly. Worth watching with some healthy skepticism.

[$1 Trillion IPO: How Anthropic's Genius Move Made it Legendary?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZgPZ6pbxuXA) - A case study on Anthropic's positioning and what made its valuation story land differently from other AI companies. Interesting from a marketing and strategy angle.

[How to completely reinvent yourself in 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P0ju8XGsYwA) - Less motivational fluff, more structured thinking about identity and change. One of those videos that's hard to describe but easy to finish.

[How Flappy Bird Ruined The Life of its Creator](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k31J6icKRUA) - I already knew the broad story, but this goes deeper than the headlines. What happened to Dong Nguyen after Flappy Bird blew up is genuinely strange and kind of sad.

[We Helped 100 Kids Fight Cancer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lVylRtlPOIE) - MrBeast. The scale of what he does for other people is hard to sit with sometimes. This is one of those videos.

[Airrack will never learn](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QlLJise0ty8) - Creator drama that's actually entertaining. Airrack keeps doing the thing, and keeps paying for it, and somehow that's watchable every time.

[I Left the Ali Abdaal Team](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zu3LQ2LXJbA) - An honest video from someone who left one of YouTube's biggest productivity brands. The behind-the-scenes picture is interesting.

[Desis Go to LONDON for the First Time](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vsOON1IDPTo) - Exactly what the title says. Funny and warm in equal measure.

[Can Tech Legends Find the Liar? (Mafia Episode 1)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EDCwQe7P8T0) - Tech founders playing Mafia. The psychology of who lies well and who doesn't is surprisingly revealing.
