## June 2026 Catch-Up | June 1–30, 2026 | 76 Articles

June was the month WordPress had a rough time and search had a worse one. The WPBeginner traffic collapse story kept me thinking for days — 2.6 million clicks down to 27K, not from one mistake but from five years of small ones compounding. And Google quietly confirmed what SparkToro's data already showed: less than a third of searches now send a click anywhere. Everything is moving toward keeping you inside the search page, and the publishers who haven't figured that out yet are going to feel it hard.

Here are the highlights from 583 links from June, what I read.

## July 2026 Catch-Up | July 1–31, 2026 | 74 Articles

July was the month everyone finally stopped pretending that traditional SEO and AI visibility are the same thing. The Moz piece on TikTokification nailed it — search is becoming a media platform, and ranking a page isn't the same as being cited in an answer. That shift runs through most of what I read this month. Also: WordPress had a genuinely interesting month, with more clear thinking about what the platform actually is than I've seen in a while.

Here are the highlights from a 512-link July reading list haul.
