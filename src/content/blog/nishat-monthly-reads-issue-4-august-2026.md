August was a lot. WordPress 7.1 launched at WordCamp US Phoenix, Google's spam update came and went, AI Overviews ate another chunk of the web, and everyone in the creator space is either panicking or doubling down. I read a lot this month, more than usual and this issue is my attempt to make sense of all of it.

As always, I saved everything in Notion (total 591 articles) and pulled the best ones here (85 articles). Grab a drink.

## 🔍 Google, AI & search

* [About Half Of Google Searches Have AI Overviews](https://www.seroundtable.com/half-google-searches-ai-overviews-41780.html) — Half. That's the number. If you're still treating AI Overviews as an edge case in your SEO thinking, now is a good time to stop.
* [What 13 Billion Google Searches Reveal About Zero-Click Behavior](https://ipullrank.com/zero-click-behavior-analysis-q3-2026) — The actual scale of zero-click is bigger than most estimates. This is the data study I kept going back to.
* [SEO vs GEO is the wrong question](https://www.jonoalderson.com/conjecture/seo-vs-geo/) — Jono Alderson cuts through the SEO vs GEO debate. The framing itself is the problem.
* [Technical SEO for the Agentic Web](https://www.advancedwebranking.com/blog/technical-seo-for-agentic-web) — Alfonso Moure on what crawling looks like when the crawler is an AI agent making decisions, not just indexing pages.
* [These SEO Strategies Drive 90% of Your AI Visibility](https://signal.zyppy.com/p/seo-strategies-for-ai-search) — Practical, not theoretical. The 90% is a bold claim but the underlying strategies hold up.
* [Content Prioritization in an AI Search Era: A Framework + Worksheet](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/content-prioritization-ai-search/) — Aleyda Solis published a full framework for deciding which content to defend, update, or let go given how AI search is changing traffic patterns.
* [Third-Party Citation Optimization Prioritization in an AI Search Era](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/third-party-citation-optimization/) — Her follow-up on getting cited by third-party sources that AI pulls from. Two must-reads from the same author this month.
* [Knowledge Graphs for SEO: How I Use Them on Content Sites](https://fajela.com/semantic-search/knowledge-graph-seo/) — Fajela with a practical breakdown. Knowledge graphs keep coming up in GEO/AI search conversations and this one explains why.
* [The Metric Most SEOs Are Still Not Measuring In The Age Of AI](https://www.seroundtable.com/ai-metric-seo-41903.html) — AI visibility is the gap most teams haven't filled yet.
* [How to Run an AI Search Visibility Audit: 8 Stages and Tools](https://searchengineland.com/ai-search-visibility-audit-484445) — The audit framework I'd use if I were doing this for a client today.
* [Zero-Click Content: The Counterintuitive Way to Succeed in a Platform-Native World](https://sparktoro.com/blog/zero-click-content/) — SparkToro's piece on giving content away on platform without expecting the click. Counterintuitive and convincing.
* [Does Your Website Still Matter in the Zero-Click Era?](https://sparktoro.com/blog/does-your-website-matter-zero-click/) — The companion piece to the one above. Yes, your site still matters. But differently.
* [When Google Stops Sending Clicks, What Still Works?](https://sparktoro.com/blog/when-google-stops-clicks/) — Amanda Natividad on the channels that hold up when organic traffic shrinks.
* [Zero-Click, AI Search, and the Last-Click Attribution Trap](https://content10x.com/zero-click-ai-search-last-click-attribution/) — Amanda Natividad in podcast form. The last-click attribution model is broken and this explains exactly how.
* [LLM traffic converts differently — here's what to do about it](https://www.leadershipinseo.com/p/llm-traffic-conversion) — AI referral traffic doesn't behave like Google referral traffic. Worth understanding before you draw conclusions from your GA numbers.
* [Shopify: AI referrals up 197%, but organic search still leads traffic](https://searchengineland.com/shopify-ai-referral-traffic-484370) — Useful data point: AI traffic is growing fast but organic isn't dead.
* [Reports Indicate Google's Spam Update Focused On SEO AI Content](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-spam-update-ai-content-41950.html) — The August spam update targeted AI-generated SEO content specifically. If your traffic dropped, this is probably why.
* [Content Effort: The Google Ranking Feature Nobody Talks About](https://searchengineland.com/content-effort-google-ranking-feature-484400) — Effort signals are real and Google is likely weighing them. Not a new idea, but finally getting traction.
* [How to Publish Citation-Worthy Content](https://www.womenintechseo.com/blog/citation-worthy-content/) — Practical steps for the kind of content that gets cited by AI, not just indexed by Google.
* [How to Report SEO & AI Visibility ROI to Leadership Who Do Not Care About Rankings](https://wordlift.io/blog/seo-ai-visibility-roi-leadership/) — The brief I wish existed when I was trying to explain organic to a CEO. Saves you from the "but what's the ranking?" conversation.
* [Reddit citations in ChatGPT fell off a cliff](https://sparktoro.com/blog/reddit-chatgpt-citations/) — Reddit's 86% drop in ChatGPT citations in August is a big story. OpenAI and Reddit are apparently pulling apart.
* [How Much of the Internet Is Written With AI? — Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/08/ai-internet-writing/) — Pew says 1 in 10 webpages now shows signs of AI authorship. This is the most-cited stat of the month.
* [Google gives publishers a new way to fight AI-driven traffic losses](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/29/google-publishers-ai-traffic/) — The Preferred Source button keeps users on-site. A small thing but publishers are paying attention.
* [A year working on content: SEO and AI impact](https://alwaysopen.design/ai-search-case-study/) — A real case study with real numbers. Rare and worth reading.
* [Misconfiguring Cloudflare Can Hurt Your SEO](https://www.seroundtable.com/cloudflare-misconfigure-seo-41882.html) — A lot of people set this wrong and don't know it.
* [How cats.txt showed llms.txt evidence is GEO astrology](https://markwilliamscook.substack.com/p/how-catstxt-showed-llmstxt-evidence) — Great debunking of the llms.txt hype. Save this for the next time someone on your team asks if you should add one.
