*A personal YouTube watchlist recap for August 2026 from Nishat Shahriyar — business, AI, WordPress drama, the MrBeast saga, and a Liziqi comeback. 37 videos worth your time.*

---

August was one of those months where the watchlist went all over the place. Business stuff, WordPress drama, MrBeast chaos, a guy trying to figure out his job, and somehow two Liziqi videos in there too. From 117 videos, here's what actually stuck.

---

## Money, business & creators

[How I Work: $40K/Month Solopreneur](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0) — The unglamorous version of running a one-person business. No montages, just actual systems and trade-offs. Worth watching if you're building something small on purpose.

[24hrs With The Happiest Billionaire, Jesse Itzler](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcNRcQkjgm8) — Jesse Itzler is genuinely one of the more interesting people to follow. This is a "day in the life" that actually shows something — not just a highlight reel.

[I spent a day with a blue-collar millionaire in NYC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LvPC_21Vboo) — The blue-collar angle is refreshing. Real money made from real work, not from content about making money.

[How to find businesses with (almost) zero competition](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qwbepa5A3xc) — Less about niches, more about how to think about market positioning. Some good mental models in here.

[Alex Hormozi's Warning: Stop Chasing AI, Build This Instead!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HwmwyBgzj8c) — Whatever you think of Hormozi, this one has a specific point — stop building on top of AI trends and start building the underlying value that AI is replacing. Good framing.

[I Debated the Inventor of Zero-Click Marketing | Amanda Natividad (SparkToro)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=klWZINduk3I) — Zero-click is the SEO conversation nobody wants to have but everyone needs to. Amanda makes the case well. The debate format keeps it honest.

[Why I Didn't Buy TED](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GulR9jLZeq0) — Behind the scenes of a major acquisition decision. Fascinating even if you don't care about TED specifically.

---

## AI, tools & how things work

[4 wild AI predictions from a $39B tech founder](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3JNYPOS2o5Q) — Some of these are actually wild. The $39B number is doing a lot of work in the title but the predictions themselves are worth hearing.

[AI Search: Why Your Whole Company Should Care | Emma Young](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4qvdNixFvCc) — Not just an SEO problem. If AI search changes how people find information, that's a product, ops, and marketing problem at the same time. Emma lays it out clearly.

[YouTube is Already 20% AI Slop](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s_JwjjNNQ_E) — Sounds hyperbolic. It's not. The numbers and examples in this one are depressing in a "yeah, we all saw this coming" kind of way.

[Should you block your Search result pages?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cfIQ_ksB61U) — A very specific SEO question with a less obvious answer than you'd expect.

[This Planet is...Up to Something](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eTfEPt96JSM) — Not AI, but genuinely fascinating. Science content that earns its runtime.

---

## WordPress & the open web

[Its been fun, WordPress.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Z2LRKeZGRA) — The vibes-based exit letter from someone leaving the WordPress ecosystem. Worth watching if you want a temperature check on where the community is.

[WordCamps Are Great, But They're Not Enough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qqLXw3jXSNA) — A fair critique. WordCamps build community but they don't necessarily move the product or the ecosystem forward. Someone had to say it.

[Imran Siddiq on Teaching WordPress, AI, Productivity, and Reinventing Life After a Heart Attack](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GRMDRpB8MiE) — More human than the title suggests. The heart attack part reframes everything else he says about how he works.

[The WordPress Agency Is Becoming Invisible: Why That Is a Good Thing | Vivek Jain](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgsxI55nBYI) — A counterintuitive take on the commodification of WordPress agencies. Worth sitting with.

[The Agentic Web Is Coming — Is WordPress Ready? | Miriam Schwab](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=35gk5eWui9E) — The most forward-looking talk in the WordCamp batch. Miriam's framing of AI agents as the new "users" is a useful lens.

[How The Ankler Outgrew Substack and Built An Independent Platform on WordPress | Nicola Catton](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ntCRNQyb6Bg) — Good case study for anyone thinking about owning their publishing infrastructure versus renting it.

## Creators & media

[We can finally talk about this.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w9UnL0FjT8U) — Withholding the context here on purpose — the title is the hook, and it pays off. Go in blind.

[Just Trying to Figure Out My Job](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ssE1rmt3EpE) — A creator being genuinely honest about not knowing what they're doing. More relatable than the usual "here's how I grew to 500k" stuff.

[The Problem With The Diary of a CEO](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=II8aZQar7XM) — A fair, specific takedown. Not a hater video — it actually diagnoses the structural issue with the format.

[The Last Honest Movie Star](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1leVuo5M6w4) — Good essay video on what "authenticity" in celebrity culture actually means right now and who still has it.

[How Netflix Ruined Korean Dramas Forever | AB Explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p1_j6izmEX4) — A real argument, not just a complaint. The money, the pacing changes, the loss of mid-budget shows — all covered.

[A Real Teacher Reacted to My Class](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AHRvUKGCfjU) — Surprisingly good. The feedback loop between content creators and actual subject-matter experts is underused.

[The Scene Every Salesman Needs to See](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DmKgsjbWw8o) — Short. You've probably seen the clip. Still worth watching with this framing around it.

[How BuzzFeed Lost the Internet Forever](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h4ITIp-pIoc) — A proper post-mortem. BuzzFeed is a cautionary tale about what happens when you optimize for traffic over audience.

## The MrBeast chapter

August brought a full MrBeast storyline that I couldn't ignore.

[Peak EXPOSES MrBeast Mansion Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1BB8wrGMgzI) — The starting point of the saga. Take it with some skepticism but the questions it raises are real.

[WE CRASHED A MRBEAST VIDEO!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CQs9j9kouRo) — Chaotic. Exactly what it sounds like. Fun internet moment.

[The Truth About Mr. Beast (Charity) Medical Videos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uSh5h2G3wI4) — The most substantive of the three. Asks harder questions about what happens when charity becomes content.
